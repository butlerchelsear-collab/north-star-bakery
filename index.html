Welcome to North Star Bakery

Since sunrise, our ovens have been warming up the neighborhood. North Star Bakery is a family-run bakery serving fresh bread, pastries, and cakes to our community every day of the week.

What We're Known For

Locals know us for our slow-fermented sourdough, our buttery morning pastries, and cakes made to order for birthdays, showers, and neighborhood celebrations. Everything is baked from scratch using simple, recognizable ingredients.

Hours & Location

Open Tuesday through Sunday, 6:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. We're located at 214 Maple Street, right next to the train stop, so commuters can grab a loaf on the way home.
