Our Story

North Star Bakery opened ten years ago in a small corner storefront with a single mixer and a dream of feeding the neighborhood well. Today we're still family-run, still mixing dough by hand, and still greeting regulars by name.

Sourcing Philosophy

We source flour from a regional mill, eggs and dairy from nearby farms, and fruit from local orchards whenever it's in season. Keeping our ingredient list short and honest is part of what makes our bread taste like home.

Staff Highlights

Our head baker, Elena, has been perfecting the Signature Loaf recipe for over a decade. Our pastry team arrives before dawn to hand-laminate croissant dough, and our counter staff know most of our regulars' usual orders by heart.
